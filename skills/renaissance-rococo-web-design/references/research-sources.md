# Research Sources

These sources establish historical distinctions, material processes, browser capabilities, and reading requirements. They are research references, not permission to reuse the images they display.

## Art history and material

- [V&A — The Rococo style: an introduction](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/the-rococo-style-an-introduction): rocaille, natural motifs, asymmetric S/C ornament, and the role of pattern books.
- [V&A — Rococo textile designs by William Kilburn](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/rococo-textile-designs-by-william-kilburn): botanical drawing, watercolor pattern design, and printed textile context.
- [V&A — The Baroque style](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/the-baroque-style): theatrical space and natural or figural ornament; useful for distinguishing Baroque from later Rococo.
- [The Wallace Collection — Fragonard's The Swing](https://www.wallacecollection.org/explore/collection/search-the-collection/les-hazards-heureux-de-lescarpolette-swing/): gesture, gaze, motion, color focus, and an emblematic Rococo work.
- [National Gallery — Perspective](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/glossary/perspective): linear perspective and the illusion of three-dimensional space.
- [National Gallery — Leonardo and The Virgin of the Rocks](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/exhibitions/past/leonardo-experience-a-masterpiece/leonardo-and-the-virgin-of-the-rocks): natural observation and aerial perspective.
- [MoMA — Henri Matisse: The Cut-Outs](https://www.moma.org/interactives/exhibitions/2014/matisse/the-cut-outs.html): gouache-painted paper, cutting, arranging, pinning, and mounting.
- [MoMA — Photomontage](https://www.moma.org/collection/terms/photomontage): photographic fragments combined into new images.
- [MoMA — Romare Bearden, Patchwork Quilt](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/79986): collage and the reworking of imagery across media.

## Contemporary case studies

- [Gucci — A Gucci Hallucination, Spring Summer 2018](https://www.gucci.com/au/en_au/st/stories/article/spring-summer-2018-ignasi-monreal): a specific campaign mixing classic-art imagery and contemporary fashion. It is not a universal Gucci design system.
- [Gucci — Julia Hetta photographs GucciHallucination](https://www.gucci.com/us/en/st/stories/article/ignasi-monreal-limited-edition-campaign): references Dutch old masters, Caravaggio, Pre-Raphaelites, and Bosch rather than one historical period.
- [Gucci — Gucci Garden](https://www.gucci.com/ie/en_gb/st/stories/inspirations-and-codes/article/gucci_garden): thematic rooms, archival objects, and curated brand history.
- [Shopify — Winter '26 Renaissance Edition](https://www.shopify.com/editions/winter2026): a contemporary web case study using Renaissance imagery and transitions alongside product content.

Brand references are used for critical study. Do not reproduce trademarks, campaign copy, distinctive props, or exact compositions.

## Web motion, layout, and accessibility

- [MDN — mask-image](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/mask-image): alpha/luminance masks and failure behavior.
- [MDN — View Transition API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/View_Transition_API): same-document and cross-document visual transitions.
- [MDN — prefers-reduced-motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@media/prefers-reduced-motion): responding to reduced-motion preferences.
- [GSAP — ScrollTrigger](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/): trigger, pin, scrub, and other scroll-sequencing capabilities.
- [Microsoft — Edge device emulation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/devtools/device-mode/): responsive viewport testing and the limits of simulation.
- [W3C — Requirements for Chinese Text Layout](https://www.w3.org/TR/clreq/): CJK punctuation, mixed text, line breaking, alignment, and related requirements.
- [W3C — WCAG 1.4.12 Text Spacing](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/text-spacing.html): compatibility with user text-spacing overrides.
- [W3C — WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html): text contrast requirements and practical caveats.
- [W3C — WCAG 1.4.10 Reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html): content reflow and exceptions for two-dimensional material.
- [W3C — WCAG 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html): control of persistent automatically moving content.
- [web.dev — Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): LCP, INP, CLS, and percentile-based field measurement.
- [The Met — Open Access](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access): an entry point for individually verified open-access collection assets.

Re-check mutable browser documentation and compatibility data during implementation. Record when a page could not be visually inspected; text extraction does not prove image analysis.
